authoritative, word-for-word version of this script** — exact spoken

| Time | Segment | Screen | Talking point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Hook and problem | Tab A `/competition` step 1 | Disconnected tools, no memory, no explanation, no evidence |
| 0:30–1:20 | Career Twin Awakening | Tab A steps 2→3 | 73% overall, 36% confidence — shown honestly, not padded; Portfolio at 59% flagged low-sample on 1 evidence item |
| 1:20–2:10 | GraphRAG root-cause reveal | Tab A steps 4→5 | Real graph traversal: wrong answer → concept → prerequisite chain → role requirement; "Graph retrieval used," 60% confidence |
| 2:10–2:50 | CARE routing and mission | Tab A steps 6→7 | Single specialist chosen dynamically at 72% confidence; mission "Strengthen FastAPI" generated from the weak evidence |
| 2:50–3:45 | Interview Replay | Tab A step 8→9, then **switch to Tab B** (pre-loaded Replay) | Real per-dimension scoring; the technical question (list comprehension) scored 100% across all six dimensions |
| 3:45–4:50 | Career Experiment Lab | Tab A step 10, then **switch to Tab B**, click "Run scenario" live | Deterministic `sim-v1` formula, not an LLM guess; disclaimer read aloud: "not a guaranteed outcome or hiring prediction" |
| 4:50–5:40 | Research proof | Tab A step 11, then **switch to Tab B**, click "Run all 6 ablations" live | CARE adaptive 100% vs. fixed baseline 12.5%; graph traversal 100% vs. vector-only 50%; one disclosed limitation (memory ablation), not hidden |
| 5:40–6:15 | Responsible AI and institutional value | Tab A steps 12→13 | The "never evaluates" list, said aloud; institutional dashboards real but honestly a cohort-of-one in this demo database |
| 6:15–7:00 | Closing | Tab A step 14 | "Your Career. Continuously Evolving." |

Rehearsal note: the two live-click moments (Experiment Lab, Research Lab)
resolve in 1–3 seconds against the deterministic engine/agents — safe to
click live in front of judges without an awkward wait. The Interview
Replay switch is the one moment that leaves Competition Mode entirely —
practice the exact Alt+Tab motion; Tab A's step position doesn't reset
when you switch away and back.
