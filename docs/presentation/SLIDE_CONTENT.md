# Slide Content

Text content for a companion slide deck (if the venue wants slides
alongside the live demo). Each slide maps to a `/competition` step where
one exists.

## Slide 1 — Title
**CareerPilot AI**
The Career Operating System
*Your Career. Continuously Evolving.*

## Slide 2 — The problem
- Disconnected tools: resume formatter, generic chatbot, static quiz
- No memory between sessions
- No explanation of *why* a student is weak
- No grounded estimate of what to do next
- Every score is a black box

## Slide 3 — The Career Twin
- One persistent, evidence-backed model per student
- Six readiness components, each with its own confidence and uncertainty
- Never a fabricated number — `insufficient_evidence` when there's no evidence
- Small-sample safeguard: one strong session ≠ an established skill

## Slide 4 — GraphRAG root cause
- Real Neo4j concept-dependency graph, relational fallback verified live
- Student → failed question → tested concept → missing prerequisite → role requirement
- Measurably more reliable than vector-only retrieval (Research Lab, Experiment B)

## Slide 5 — CARE: confidence-aware routing
- 6 real routes, chosen per-decision from real evidence factors
- Not a fixed pipeline — proven with live route diversity
- Escalates to human review when confidence stays low

## Slide 6 — Autonomous improvement loop
- Observe → Diagnose → Plan → Teach → Assess → Reflect → Update Twin
- A missed question becomes a mission with concrete tasks and a resource
- The loop closes with a named-evidence explanation, not a vague update

## Slide 7 — Interview Arena
- 6 modes, voice or typed (never blocked by mic denial)
- 5 specialist evaluation agents, CARE decides which subset runs
- Resume-claim evidence checking — respectful, never an accusation

## Slide 8 — Interview Replay
- Audio, transcript, timeline markers derived from real transcript features
- Evidence checks, agent scores, agreement, confidence
- Better-answer framework, related Career Twin skills

## Slide 9 — Career Experiment Lab
- Deterministic, versioned simulation engine (`sim-v1`)
- Diminishing returns, evidence diversity, historical trend, role importance
- "Personalized scenario estimate — not a guaranteed outcome"

## Slide 10 — Research Benchmark Lab
- CARE-adaptive vs. fixed routing — real agreement rates
- Graph traversal vs. vector-only retrieval — honestly labeled
- Calibration: Brier score, ECE, reliability bins, from real data

## Slide 11 — Responsible AI
- No hiring probability. No lie detection. No public ranking.
- Full self-service data export, audio deletion, account deletion
- Every score traces to a model/formula/policy version

## Slide 12 — Institutional scale
- Faculty: cohort skill gaps, privacy-safe
- Placement: readiness distribution, program effectiveness
- Recruiter: opt-in only, no automated hiring call
- Admin: real service health, CARE analytics
