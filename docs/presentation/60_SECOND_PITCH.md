# 60-Second Pitch

> "Every student today uses disconnected tools — a resume formatter here, a
> generic AI chatbot for interview practice there — and none of them
> remember you, or explain *why* you're weak, or tell you what to actually
> do next.
>
> CareerPilot AI is different. It builds one living Career Twin per
> student, backed entirely by real evidence — never an invented score. When
> it finds a weakness, it doesn't guess — it traces the actual root cause
> through a real knowledge graph: this question, this concept, this
> missing prerequisite, this role requirement. Then it dynamically decides
> how much AI reasoning the decision deserves — one specialist, a full
> multi-agent council, or a flag for human review — and shows you exactly
> which route it took and why, in a Trust Center, not a black box.
>
> It evaluates voice interviews with the same evidence discipline, checks
> whether your claims are actually backed by your resume — respectfully,
> never accusing you of lying — and lets you simulate 'what if I spent my
> next 20 hours on SQL instead of communication practice' with a
> deterministic formula, not an LLM making up a number.
>
> And we can prove it's better than one generic chatbot — a live Research
> Lab, real experiments, real numbers, run in front of you right now."

Opening slide of Competition Mode (`/competition`, step 1) for the visual.
