# Winning Live Demo Story

## Demo Persona

**Aarav**, a student targeting a Data Analyst role.

## Scripted Journey

1. Aarav uploads a resume and selects a target job description.
2. CareerPilot creates a Career Twin from resume evidence and a baseline assessment.
3. Career OS shows an overall readiness score with confidence and evidence.
4. ATS Intelligence detects weak SQL evidence.
5. GraphRAG identifies JOINs and relational algebra as the root cause.
6. CARE explains why retrieval and multiple specialists were invoked.
7. Career OS generates a short SQL mission.
8. Aarav completes a targeted assessment.
9. Career Twin updates from 43% to 58%, with exact evidence shown.
10. Aarav attends a voice mock interview.
11. Technical, communication, and resume-evidence evaluators produce scores.
12. Interview Replay highlights a strong section and an unsupported claim.
13. Trust Center shows agent agreement, evidence, confidence, and decision trace.
14. Experiment Lab compares 20 hours of SQL, DSA, communication, or a mixed plan.
15. The simulated Career Twin animates the best scenario.

## Closing Line

CareerPilot does not merely generate career advice. It builds a living evidence-backed Career Twin, diagnoses root causes, selects the appropriate level of AI reasoning, tests alternative improvement paths, and continuously learns which intervention works best for each student.
